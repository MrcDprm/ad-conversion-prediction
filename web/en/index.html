How it works

Plain logistic regression can only draw a straight boundary and falls behind. Adding the squares and the product of age and salary bends the boundary, and the simple model matches the complex ones. Switch between models on the map to see the difference.

See the model comparison

Data and limits

The data is a teaching dataset of 400 people widely used in machine learning courses: gender, age, estimated salary and whether they bought the advertised product. It is not data from a real campaign; the results show how the methods work.

Gender is left out of the model: it adds nothing to the prediction here, and using it to target ads raises a fairness question.

The age and salary you enter are not sent or stored anywhere; the prediction runs in your browser.
